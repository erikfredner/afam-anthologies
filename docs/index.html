African American Anthologies

Interactive figures from an analysis of author and work selection across twenty-six anthologies of African American literature published between 1929 and 2025, including the four editions of The Norton Anthology of African American Literature.

Figures

  • Author vs. work retention, by edition pair
    For every ordered pair of anthologies, the share of the earlier one's works and authors that reappear in the later one. Points sit above the diagonal when authors are retained more reliably than the particular works chosen to represent them — which is nearly always. Hover any point to see which two anthologies it compares, in which direction, and the counts behind each share; click a legend entry to isolate a category.

Source and analysis code: github.com/erikfredner/afam-anthologies
