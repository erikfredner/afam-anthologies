African American Anthologies

Figures and tables from an analysis of author and work selection across twenty-six anthologies of African American literature published between 1929 and 2025, including the four editions of The Norton Anthology of African American Literature.

Figures

  • Author vs. work retention, by edition pair
    For every ordered pair of anthologies, the share of the earlier one's works and authors that reappear in the later one. Points sit above the diagonal when authors are retained more reliably than the particular works chosen to represent them — which is nearly always. Hover any point to see which two anthologies it compares, in which direction, and the counts behind each share; click a legend entry to isolate a category.

Tables

  • Author selection records
    All 575 authors ever selected for one of the twenty-six anthologies, with their dates, the share of anthologies that chose them, and the share of the anthologies published after their first appearance that chose them again. Sorted by selection record; click any column heading to re-sort.
  • Work selection records
    The same for particular works, all 3,236 of them. Excerpts and individual poems count in their own right rather than being folded into the book they come from, which the In column names. The 298 works the anthologies print without attribution have an empty Author cell rather than a credit to “Anonymous.”
